Maths practice that is fresh, adaptive and open

Tenali is for Class 4 and above. There are strong maths platforms already. Many need an account or a subscription, and a teacher cannot change them. Tenali takes a different path.

Try it

A tiny taste of the idea. Get one right and the next one gets a little harder. Get one wrong and you get a hint.

What makes Tenali different

93

Puzzles, not drills

93 kinds of puzzles across 69 topics, so practice feels like solving something and not like repeating a worksheet.

↻

A fresh question every time

Questions are generated by the program, so a child cannot memorise the answers and has to understand the idea.

↗

Difficulty that adapts

The level moves with the child, so it is neither too easy to be dull nor too hard to give up on.

✓

Every answer explained

After each question the child can see the solution step by step, so a mistake becomes something to learn from.

</>

Open to everyone

Each puzzle is a small piece of code. Students and teachers can read it, fix it, or add a new one.

★

Coming in version 2 Planned

Topics arranged by class from simple to complex, and a short game that finds where a child should start.

Why it follows FLN

A child who is shaky on counting in Class 2 struggles with fractions in Class 5. FLN finds and fixes those early gaps. Tenali carries the same idea on: find the gap, practise it, check it again.
