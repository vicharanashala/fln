One learning path, from Balvatika to Class 12

Learning gaps that start in the first years follow a child for the rest of school. We are rebuilding two linked platforms so a teacher can see where each child stands and what to teach next, at every stage.

FLN

Balvatika to Class 3 · foundational literacy and numeracy

Small children are not tested like older students. A teacher prints a worksheet, observes the child, and enters the result. The platform places each child on a level and shows a simple band: Emerging, Developing or Secure.

Open FLN v2

Tenali

Class 4 and above · practice and mastery

Once children can read and count, they need practice that adapts to them, topic by topic, from simple to complex. Tenali carries the same idea forward: find the gap, practise it, check it again.

Why both stages matter

A child who is shaky in Class 2 reading or number sense struggles in Class 5 maths and in every subject after it. Fixing the early years without a path onward loses the gain; building the later years without a foundation builds on sand. Each platform on its own answers half the question, so we present them as one path.

How we move forward

  1. Stabilise. Both platforms are rebuilt on a clean base, with FLN first and Tenali next, so that what a school sees is dependable.
  2. Check with real content. The question banks are reviewed with subject experts before any child is assessed.
  3. Pilot in a school. Start with one school, record where children begin, and track them across the term.
  4. Extend upward. Add the remaining classes only after the earlier stage works in a real classroom.
