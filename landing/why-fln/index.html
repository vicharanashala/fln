Five ideas behind FLN

✎

Paper first

The child writes with a pencil on a printed worksheet. No phone or tablet for the child. The teacher records the result.

109

A level map, not just a mark

Every child is placed on one of 109 levels across 24 skills, so the next step is a specific skill, such as counting or place value.

👁

Made for the youngest children

Balvatika children cannot always write. Teachers can record what they observe, such as counting objects aloud, alongside what is written.

●

Three plain words

Each skill is shown as Emerging, Developing or Secure. A teacher can read it in a second and explain it to a parent.

</>

Open for anyone to inspect and improve

The levels and the code are public on GitHub. Teachers, researchers and students can read them, question them and add to them.

Who it is for

A class teacher with thirty children at different levels and no time to work out each child's gap by hand. FLN keeps the classroom as it is, with paper and a pencil, and gives the teacher a clear next step for each child.
