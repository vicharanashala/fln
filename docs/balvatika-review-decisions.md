# Balvatika question bank: review decisions

Source: the review of the "Balvatika Question/Observation Bank" (290 draft items, 29 concepts) by the curriculum reviewer, comments of 22 October and replies of 31 October 2026, including the three-concept pilot (L19, L20, L31). This page records what was decided so that v2 content is built to it. It is a record of decisions, not an approval of any item: the bank is still a draft and each item needs its own sign-off (`status: approved`) before a teacher can print it.

## Assessment design
- **Five questions per concept.** Papers cover as many concepts as the teacher chooses, five questions each. (Replaces the earlier ten per concept.)
- **Bands** for every concept, on the five-question score: 0-1 **Emerging**, 2-3 **Developing**, 4-5 **Secure**. One mark per correct answer, 0 otherwise. Prompts and support are recorded separately from the mark.
  - Emerging: not yet able to show the skill consistently, even with support, or just beginning to show awareness.
  - Developing: can show the skill with a prompt, some guidance, concrete materials or extra support.
  - Secure: shows the skill independently and consistently across the tasks or in familiar situations.
- **Two kinds of evidence.** Written worksheet performance plus teacher observation. Observation codes: Independent (completes without help), Prompted (completes after a verbal prompt), Supported (needs demonstration, objects or help), Strategy (counts all, counts on, uses fingers, guesses).
- **Five-question design:** two on the core skill, two on the same skill in a different context or format, one on application or extension where it fits.
- **Unclear evidence:** give that child one individual follow-up activity. Do not give every child more questions.
- **Teacher chooses the concepts** for baseline, mid-year and end of year. There is no fixed set.
- **Recording results:** by scanning the filled sheet (typed entry stays as the fallback).
- **Observation sheet:** include the fixed line "Maths vocabulary: uses words such as more, fewer, longer, shorter, first and last correctly."
- **Literacy** (letters and sounds) is not part of this sheet; it belongs to the literacy module.
- **After Balvatika:** Class 1, 2 and 3 are reviewed in the same way. The order is still to be decided.

## Question rules
- Answering by **circling** is enough; a child who can write may write. This applies to the numeral, counting and combining concepts.
- **Pictures of objects** go in a single row with clear gaps; if they do not fit, use two rows. Never scatter them.
- **Number matching (L20):** one table of five numerals against five object groups, each correct line one mark. Use both Table A (1 to 5) and Table B (6 to 10). Groups are in a different order from the numerals.
- **L19 question 5 ("one more than")** is kept as a distractor type, with a note to the teacher: it checks whether the child can take the concept a level up, and a child who answers questions 1 to 4 correctly does not necessarily lack the concept; the teacher can check with prompting.
- **L31 combine groups:** circle the total from three numerals; totals 3, 5, 7, 8 and 9 rising in order; record how the child finds the total.
- Where a question says "up to", give the exact number (for example exactly 10 elephants).
- Compare **two** objects, not three, for longer/shorter. Use "longest", not "tallest", where the question asks for the longest.
- Do not show numerals under object groups when the question is about comparing objects (the child could compare the numerals instead).
- Avoid choices that make the answer too obvious (for example a watermelon against a grape).
- A pattern question must say whether the pattern is by colour or by shape.

## Items to rewrite or leave out
- **Sequencing (L8):** observation based on the child's arrangement of cards, with teacher prompts (give the cards; "what do you do first?"; "what do you do after waking up?"; first, next, last). Use routines every child knows. Rewrite the bus-to-school, nap-after-play, roti-making (kitchen), umbrella-in-rain and egg-to-hen items; show brushing teeth so it can come before or after a bath; plant growth as seed, small plant, big plant, tree. Example given: washing hands, taking a plate, eating, putting the plate away.
- **Re-sorting** tasks cannot be put on a worksheet; observation only.
- **Cannot be assessed on paper:** weight; tracing a face of a 3D object.
- **Grade 1 concepts, not Balvatika:** L35 (creates a new pattern), L36 (describes a pattern's rule), L37 (describes 3D solids in own words), L38 (traces faces of 3D objects). L40 (compares capacity of two vessels) is acceptable for Grade 1; equal quantity must be stated.
- **Days of the week and months (L41):** taught by rote. Replace "birthday month" with a month the children know, for example the month after April.
- **Writes numerals (L30):** state "numerals from 1 to 9".
- **Other wording:** replace written answers with circling where the child may not be able to write; use NCERT and Unmukh questions as a reference for age-appropriate items.
