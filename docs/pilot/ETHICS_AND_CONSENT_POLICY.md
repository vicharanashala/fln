# Pilot Ethics Clearance & Guardian/School Consent Policy

**Document Status:** DRAFT, for review by the Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC)  
**Target Group:** Foundational Literacy & Numeracy (FLN) Students (Pilot begins with Balvatika / foundational stage; exact grade and age ranges to be confirmed with project leads per #388)  
**Governing Institution:** Educational Research Team & Partner School Systems  
**Issue Reference:** #452  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The FLN Assessment & Personalized Worksheet Platform evaluates foundational numeracy through printed worksheets, digitizing physical completed papers via photography, and applying automated ICR/OCR and human diagnostic review. 

Because the pilot involves photographing handwritten papers produced by minors in school environments, this draft policy establishes:
1. **Institutional Ethics Compliance:** Framework prepared for submission to the Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) adhering to national research guidelines for children.
2. **Guardian & School Consent:** Standardized bilingual consent forms (English & Hindi) for school administrators and parents/guardians incorporating Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 children's data provisions.
3. **Data Protection & PII Masking:** Anonymization protocols requiring student Aadhaar and personal identifier masking before digital ingestion.
4. **Planned Data Lifecycle:** Guidelines for photo access, retention limits, and withdrawal/deletion procedures.

No worksheet photograph or student response data may be collected in any classroom until formal IEC clearance and signed consent are secured under this policy.

---

## 2. Institutional Ethics Framework (IEC Submission Draft)

In accordance with institutional requirements for research involving minors:
- **Risk Assessment:** Minimal risk. Participation involves normal classroom instructional and assessment activities (completing math worksheets).
- **Voluntary Participation:** Participation is strictly voluntary. A child or parent may refuse consent or withdraw at any time without any impact on regular academic standing, grades, or school benefits.
- **Child Assent:** Verbal assent is obtained from participating children in age-appropriate language prior to administering pilot worksheets.
- **Ethics Review Status:** Draft protocol prepared for submission to the Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC); formal approval not yet requested. Data collection will be restricted strictly to pedagogical assessment and error-pattern identification.

## 5. Data Protection, Privacy & Planned Technical Controls

### 5.1 Aadhaar & PII Masking
- **Immediate Protocol:** Before any paper worksheet photo is captured, student Aadhaar numbers and identifiable names on paper headers must be physically covered or masked.
- **Planned Automated Control:** Automated digital PII masking and redaction in the upload/OCR pipeline is planned as an engineering enhancement (tracked for implementation in future pipeline milestones).

### 5.2 Storage & Security Controls
- **Access Boundary:** Student records and evaluation data must adhere to strict role-based access scoping (`canAccessStudent` enforcing school-level boundaries).
- **Planned Infrastructure Control:** Migration of digitized assets to dedicated encrypted cloud object storage (e.g. GCS with strict KMS policies) is planned for production deployment; pilot stages run in local restricted environments.
- **No Commercial Use:** Photographs and student records are never sold, shared with commercial third parties, or used for non-educational purposes.

### 5.3 Planned Retention & Deletion Policy
- **Planned Audit Retention:** Raw worksheet photos are planned to be retained for up to 180 days following pilot evaluation to allow human diagnostic calibration (#410), after which raw images will be purged.
- **Planned Erasure Workflow:** Automated deletion workflows to guarantee complete purge within 14 calendar days of a consent withdrawal will be built as an administrative tool.
